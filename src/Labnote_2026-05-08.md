## 7. Conclusion
- The new enhanced variants did not improve over baseline AUROC 0.8025.
- Best enhanced result in this round is V1 AUROC 0.6509, which is 0.1516 below baseline.
- Current best model remains TCNBiGRUTransformer from 2026-05-01.

## 8. Observations
- More capacity did not help under this low-positive regime (30 train positives, 14 test positives).
- Validation AUPRC improvements did not translate to better test AUROC in these enhanced settings.
- Operating point behavior changed substantially across variants (very low threshold in V1 vs conservative threshold in V2), but ranking quality on test remained lower than baseline.

## 9. Next practical step
- Keep the baseline architecture and perform conservative ablations one change at a time (for example: only deepen TCN while keeping pooling and Transformer unchanged), with multiple seeds to reduce variance before claiming improvements.
